# 007 scope, fullscreen and log cleanup, 2026-10-07

The owner narrowed active title work to Quantum of Solace, Blood Stone and
007 Legends. FIFA Street, NHL and original Xbox execution are outside this
work queue. These are Xbox 360 titles and retain the Xbox 360 Guide.

The preceding PPC regression runs explicitly passed `--fullscreen=false` and
launched each title once on its previous build and once on the candidate.
The Windows title bar came from that test override; the SDK already defaults
to fullscreen. Paired runs compared startup behavior across a runtime/codegen
change. Routine checks now use one candidate instance per title; comparisons
remain appropriate when required by a specific regression.

`scripts/Launch-007.ps1` makes borderless fullscreen, direct launch and
warnings/errors logging explicit. Twenty-two existing local settings files
(built executable configs and per-title LocalAppData configs) received the
same presentation/logging settings. Other preferences, including patch choices,
were preserved. No shared runtime change or game regeneration was needed.

## Log cleanup

At the owner's request, 995 existing work/game log files were removed from the
local SDK, Guide, title workspaces and their known LocalAppData log folders,
reclaiming 565,587,319 bytes (539.39 MiB). Only checked `.log`/numbered `.log`
paths were removed. Tracked source, game content, saves, shader caches, binaries
and small result/hash summaries were retained. The additional logs from the
fullscreen check were removed after recording the results.

Historical records retain their reported results and hashes, but their raw
log paths are no longer available. New investigations must capture fresh,
bounded diagnostics rather than claim to inspect those deleted logs.

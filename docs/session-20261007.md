## Log cleanup

local SDK, Guide, title workspaces and their known LocalAppData log folders,
reclaiming 565,587,319 bytes (539.39 MiB). Only checked `.log`/numbered `.log`

and small result/hash summaries were retained. The additional logs from the
fullscreen check were removed after recording the results.

Historical records retain their reported results and hashes, but their raw
log paths are no longer available. New investigations must capture fresh,
bounded diagnostics rather than claim to inspect those deleted logs.
